## Tools & Projects

### [local-cyber-feed](https://github.com/benjamin-wulf/local-cyber-feed)
A locally hosted RSS reader and news aggregator, intended to act as a lightweight server with client access via browser.<br>Why bring another RSS reader into the world when there are already powerful and free options? Because sometimes something simple and straightforward is nice to use (*and I'd rather dissapear into the woods before adding another monthly subscription to my life*).
